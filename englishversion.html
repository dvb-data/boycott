Boycott List Checker

This dashboard was created to consolidate data published by Burma Campaign UK and the Ministry of Planning, Finance and Investment (MOPFI) of the National Unity Government (NUG), aiming to allow users to easily search and view information all in one place.

Military-Linked Business: Compiles products, brands, and services that are owned, managed, or controlled by the Myanmar military or military-owned conglomerates.

The Dirty List: Features international companies that directly or indirectly support the Myanmar military with funds, arms, technology, or other supplies, as well as companies involved in projects associated with human rights violations and environmental damage.
